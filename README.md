# Health-Care-Analysis
Analysis for Optimal Patient Care


EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This report analyzes 54,965 admission records to describe patient demographics, medical conditions, billing patterns, hospital admissions, length of stay, medications, doctors, test results, and monthly trends. The average recorded length of stay was 15 days, the average patient age was 52 years, and the average billing amount was $25,544.31. Arthritis had the highest number of records, with 9218, while Diabetes had the highest total billing at $236.49 million. These findings describe patterns in the dataset; they do not, on their own, prove that a treatment was effective or that a hospital was more efficient.

OBJECTIVE
This report analyzes patient trends, hospital activity, and medical insights to identify patterns that may help hospitals improve patient care, manage costs, and review service efficiency.

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) 
•	Compare the average length of stay by admission type.
•	Identify the medical condition with the highest number of admissions.
•	Compare total billing by medical condition and identify the three highest.
•	Summarize patient records by blood group and gender.
•	Identify hospitals with the highest admission counts.
•	Compare the average age of patients across medical conditions.
Deep Analysis & Insights 
•	Identify the insurance provider with the highest total billing.
•	Find the most frequently recorded medication for each medical condition.
•	Identify the doctor with the highest number of patient records.
•	Summarize test results overall and across medical conditions.
•	Review monthly and yearly admission patterns.
